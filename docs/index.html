Calm room

A fire that burns down on its own, a place you can return to, and sand you can rake. Nothing here scores you.

Sit down
Status Built and working offline: a fire whose size is a pure function of the days since you last tended it — monotonic, never rising on its own, never locked, never punished; a landscape generated from a seed so the same seed is the same place; sand you can rake and stones you can place, counted by nothing.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: sound. A fire could have a crackle; there is none here, and the app does not pretend to have one.

What it does

  • A fire that burns down while you are away and comes back to full when you tend it.
  • A landscape generated from a seed — return to the same seed and it is the same place.
  • Sand you can rake and stones you can place. It settles. Nothing is counted.

What it will not do

  • Punish you for leaving. No streak to break, no lock, no lost state — only a smaller fire when you come back.
  • Score anything. No goal, no points, no ending, in any of the three rooms.
  • Relight an unreadable fire at full strength. That would erase the only consequence the app exists to create; it is reported and offered for export instead.
  • Count a tend that did not happen. A tap that is not an interaction is refused, and the app says the fire was not tended.
  • Use a key or a network. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA tend with no interaction is not counted. A palette that does not parse is refused with the value shown, and the previous scene stays on screen.
No networkNothing changes: the fire, the seed and the garden are on the device.
Storage full or unavailableThe state is not written and the app says the fire's history will not survive — it is never shown as tended when the write failed.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; the fire is not quietly relit at full strength.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js — the spec's countable “done means” is asserted there.
