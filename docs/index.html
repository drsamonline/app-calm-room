Why there is no streak

A streak turns a bad week into a loss. This app counts nothing about you: not visits, not minutes, not consecutive days.

What it refuses

No notifications, no nudges, no “we miss you”. If it is closed, it is silent — and opening it again is not a fresh start, just a return.
