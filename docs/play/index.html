Calm room

A fire that burns down on its own, a place you can return to, and sand you can rake. Nothing here scores you.

about

Size is a function of the days since you last tended it, and of nothing else — so it cannot rise on its own and refreshing the page will not help it. It burns down while you are away and nothing is taken from you. There is no streak to break, no lock, and no penalty beyond the fire being smaller when you come back.

A view, not a screensaver: return to the same seed and it is the same place. No goal, no score, no ending.

Drag to rake. Nothing is counted.
Calm room keeps the fire, the seed and the garden on this device. No network, no account, no score, no streak to break.
